# Faculty List

## 1. Professor Lei Tian — ECE/BME

**Relevant Work:** “Reliable deep learning-based phase imaging with uncertainty quantification,” Optica, 2019.

**Why Relevant:** Professor Tian works on computational imaging, machine learning, and uncertainty-aware image reconstruction. His work on pixel-level uncertainty estimation is closely related to our goal of providing spatial fidelity information alongside low-light enhancement.

**Question:** In a low-light enhancement system where paired normal-light references may be imperfect, what is the most convincing way to validate whether a spatial uncertainty or fidelity indicator reflects reconstruction reliability rather than image brightness or model sensitivity?

## 2. Professor Vivek Goyal — ECE

**Relevant Project:** “Low-Light 3D Imaging: From Fundamental Limits to Practical Systems.”

**Why Relevant:** Professor Goyal studies computational imaging, inverse problems, and photon-efficient imaging, including reconstruction when very little light is available. This is directly related to the measurement limitations underlying low-light enhancement.

**Question:** In photon-limited imaging, how should we distinguish uncertainty caused by insufficient measurements from errors introduced by the reconstruction algorithm? Could a useful fidelity indicator be derived from the measurement model rather than only from perturbing an enhancement model?
